## Private disclosure

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately to `i.am@dangkhoa.dev`. Include the affected revision, reproduction steps, expected impact, and sanitized evidence. Do not include real tokens or private voice samples.

The public tunnel is considered safe for this demo only when coordinator authentication is enabled. Ports 8091 and 8092 are internal worker boundaries and must not be exposed directly.
